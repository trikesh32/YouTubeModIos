Own implementation by trikesh32 contributors, 2026. MIT.
Generic Mach-O injection and metadata helpers adapted from trikesh32/TikTokModIos (MIT).
No YTLite/YouTube Plus source or binary is included. Prior technical research informed requirements; this is not claimed to be strict clean-room work.
Apple/Google app binaries, signing material and account data are not part of this repository.
Third-party features will carry their own licenses when integrated.

Playback/menu research reference: itzzace/ytkace (MIT), commit
5f8cb77bbd50249d2561a129afd3bb04313568ee. Read BackgroundPlaybackHooks.mm,
PlaybackFixHooks.mm, NativeSettingsEntry.mm and SettingsEntry.mm while comparing
approaches. No source from these files was incorporated in 0.1.2. The four new
background getter signatures were independently checked in the local YouTube
21.46.3 Objective-C metadata. This research does not establish runtime behavior.
